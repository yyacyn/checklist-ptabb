**SCORING**

The granted scores should reflect the following:

- **5 “excellent” **means that the auditee’s conformance with SMS is outstanding and/or the rate of SMS implementation within the department is maintained at a level much higher than is usually expected.

- **4 “above expectations” **means that the auditee’s conformance with SMS is high and/or the rate of SMS implementation within the department is significantly better than what would normally scored as acceptable.

- **3 “satisfactory”** means that the auditee’s conformance with SMS is in line with normal expectation and/or the rate of SMS implementation within the department is satisfactory.

- **2 “fair”** means that the auditee’s conformance with SMS is below expectations and/or the rate of SMS implementation within the department is not satisfactory but with the proper training in certain areas the auditees can be in satisfactory level.

- **1** **“unsatisfactory” **means that the auditee’s capacity is inadequate in the area(s) being audited and/or the rate of SMS implementation within the department is poor and the possibility for the persons to be sufficiently improved, even with additional training, the improvement is uncertain.
